# T4SKAG3NT 0.1.9 Background Model Downloads

This update keeps setup moving while optional local models download:

- Allows setup to continue while sub-agent models download in the background.
- Lets users move through setup while a newly selected coordination model downloads; final readiness is checked before startup.
- Keeps incomplete sub-agent models out of task routing until their downloads are complete and verified.
- Automatically resumes configured partial model downloads after setup or an app relaunch.
- Preserves visible download progress and model roles on the AI models setup page.
